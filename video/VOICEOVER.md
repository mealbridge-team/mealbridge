# Voiceover script (about 2:45)

Read by the ElevenLabs premade "Eric" voice, which works on the free plan (`node make-voice.mjs`, voice id cjVigY5qzO86Huf0OWal). Mike, the Bouncer voice, is a library voice and needs a paid plan: `node make-voice.mjs zfc8CJQpe8Kqyl0vvCzo`. Each line starts with its scene; the narration adds to what's on screen rather than reading it out.

| # | Scene | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | Every night, restaurants in the Bay Area throw away good, cooked food. A few miles away, a shelter is short of dinner. |
| 2 | The problem | The food isn't the problem. The last mile is. Someone has to check it's safe, find a shelter that can use it, and drive it there. And the volunteer who drives usually pays for the fuel. |
| 3 | Reveal | This is MealBridge. AI agents handle the messy parts, plain code handles the money and the food safety, and PayPal makes sure no volunteer drives for free. |
| 4 | Guided rescue | Anyone can try it. Start the guided rescue, and a bar at the bottom walks you through one delivery, switching you to each person in turn. |
| 5 | Restaurant posts | The restaurant posts tonight's surplus, as a photo or a note in any language. Gemini reads the dishes, portions and allergens. Fixed food-safety rules decide if it can go, and the matcher picks a shelter that can use it tonight. |
| 6 | Funded first | Before anyone moves, the driver's fuel money is reserved. Restaurants never pay per pickup. A trip is paid from the restaurant's compliance plan on PayPal Subscriptions, then local donors, then sponsors and city grants. |
| 7 | Driver | Maya, a volunteer, sees her reimbursement before she starts. She photographs the food at pickup, drives it over, and photographs it again at the shelter. Reusing the pickup photo won't work. A safeguard catches it. |
| 8 | Verified payout | The shelter taps Received. Now three signals agree: the photo, the GPS, and the shelter's tap. Only then does PayPal Payouts reimburse Maya, to PayPal or Venmo. If anything doesn't match, a person checks it. Never an automatic yes. |
| 9 | Donors and sponsors | When a restaurant isn't on a plan, its trip waits for funding. Neighbours fund it with PayPal Checkout, and sponsors top up the fund with PayPal invoices. Our nudge agent drafts the reminders. |
| 10 | PayPal loop | Four PayPal products in one loop: Subscriptions, Checkout, Invoicing and Payouts. And the AI never moves a dollar on its own. |
| 11 | Impact | Every meal, mile and dollar is public. The routes on the map are real deliveries, the charts are built with AG Studio, and an insights agent answers questions from the live ledger. |
| 12 | Outro | MealBridge. Tonight's surplus, on a shelter's table, and no volunteer out of pocket. |
