# Demo video script (target 2:45, hard limit under 3:00)

Record at 1440×900 in the browser, one tab per screen, with the demo freshly reset (Coordinator →
Reset the demo data). Use royalty-free background music or none. Speak calmly; each scene lists the
voice-over and what to show.

## 0:00–0:15 · The problem

**Show:** the landing page, slow scroll from the headline to "How a rescue works".

**Say:** "Every night, Bay Area restaurants bin cooked food a few miles from shelters that need it.
The food isn't the problem. The last mile is: deciding where it goes, checking it's safe, and making
sure the volunteer who drives it isn't out of pocket."

## 0:15–0:40 · A restaurant posts food

**Show:** Try the demo, Start the guided rescue (the guide bar appears at the bottom). Restaurant screen (phone width). Take a photo of real food, or type "about 30 portions
of veg biryani, kept hot, ready till 10pm". Press Post surplus. Pause on the result card.

**Say:** "Lakeside Catering snaps a photo. Gemini reads the dishes, portions and allergens. Plain-code
food-safety rules decide if it can go, and the Matcher picks a shelter that can use it tonight. Then
the Treasurer reserves the driver's fuel money before anyone moves, here from the restaurant's own
compliance plan, a PayPal subscription."

## 0:40–1:10 · The driver and the live map

**Show:** The guide ticks step 1; press Continue as Maya R. Driver screen: the job, "$4 reserved for you". Press Demo: simulate
the drive, add a photo, I've picked it up, then Delivered at the shelter. Press Continue as the shelter:
the car on the live map with an ETA.

**Say:** "Maya, a volunteer, gets the job with the reimbursement already reserved. The shelter
watches the food arrive live. Confidential shelters are never shown exactly on a public map."

## 1:10–1:35 · Verification and the payout

**Show:** Shelter taps Received. Coordinator screen: the trip moves to "Driver paid". Optionally show
the PayPal sandbox payout in the driver's sandbox account.

**Say:** "A payout needs three signals to agree: Gemini checks the drop-off photo, GPS puts the driver
at the shelter, and the shelter taps once. Then PayPal Payouts reimburses Maya to PayPal or Venmo,
before she's home. If anything doesn't match, it goes to a person, never an automatic yes."

## 1:35–2:00 · Where the money comes from

**Show:** Donate page: "Waiting for funding", fund a waiting trip with PayPal Checkout. Sponsors page:
the fund, the rescues it paid for, and "Get a PayPal invoice" to top up. Coordinator console: press
"Draft a friendly reminder" on an unpaid invoice and show the Nudge agent's text.

**Say:** "Restaurants never pay per pickup. Trips are funded by the restaurant's plan, then donors who
choose a waiting rescue, then sponsors and city grants billed with PayPal invoices. Our Nudge agent
writes reminders from real impact: thirty-one trips, a thousand meals."

## 2:00–2:35 · Impact, with AG Studio

**Show:** Impact page. Numbers count up; scroll to the map as the routes draw in. Click Oakland in the
AG Studio filter to cross-filter the charts. Ask the Insights agent "Which area relies most on
donors?"

**Say:** "Every meal, mile and dollar is public. Routes on the map are real deliveries, the charts are
built with AG Studio, and our Insights agent answers from the live ledger without inventing a
figure."

## 2:35–2:50 · Close

**Show:** back to the landing page stats.

**Say:** "MealBridge: agents for the messy parts, plain code for the money and the food safety, and
PayPal to make sure no volunteer drives for free."
